# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid model
The standard working model is hybrid: 3 days in the office and 2 days remote each week. Teams agree which days are office days, and Tuesday and Wednesday are the default anchor days for all teams.

## Eligibility
Remote work becomes available after the employee has completed the 3-month probation period. During probation, employees work from the office on all days unless their manager agrees otherwise.

## Fully remote roles
Fully remote working is available only with approval from the Vice President of the employee's department. It is reviewed every 12 months, and the employee may be asked to attend the office up to 4 days per quarter for team events.

## Core hours
Regardless of location, employees must be available during core hours, from 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours, working times are flexible by agreement with the line manager.

## Home office support
Employees working remotely receive a one-time home office stipend of $400 for a desk, chair or similar items. In addition, the company pays $40 per month toward internet costs. Both are claimed through payroll and do not use the expense process.

## Working from abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. Approval is needed from both HR and the Tax team at least 30 days beforehand, because of tax and legal implications. Working abroad without approval is a policy breach.

## Security requirements
Remote employees must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal systems, and must not work on company data over public Wi-Fi without the VPN. Calls with confidential content should be taken in private spaces.
