# Northwind Facilities Information Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts, including email, VPN and code repositories. Employees must use the company-approved password manager and must never share passwords, even with colleagues or IT staff.

## Devices
Laptops must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. All company laptops use full-disk encryption, which must not be disabled. USB storage devices are blocked by default, and exceptions need written approval from the Security team.

## Data classification
Company data is classified into 4 levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details and employee health records, may only be stored in approved systems and must never be sent by personal email or messaging apps.

## Phishing
Suspicious emails should be forwarded to phishing@helpdesk.example and then deleted. The Security team runs simulated phishing tests every quarter. An employee who fails 3 simulated tests within 12 months must repeat the security awareness training.

## Access control
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis. Managers review their team's access rights every quarter, and access for leavers is removed on their last working day. Shared accounts are not allowed.

## Security training
All employees must complete security awareness training within 7 days of joining and then once every 12 months. Training completion is tracked, and access may be suspended if the annual refresher is more than 30 days overdue.

## Guest and public networks
Visitors must use the guest Wi-Fi network, which is isolated from internal systems. Company devices should not be connected to unknown public networks without the VPN.

## Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported as described in the Incident Reporting Policy.
