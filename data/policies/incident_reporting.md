# Northwind Facilities Incident Reporting Policy

## What counts as an incident
An incident is any event that harms, or could harm, people, data or systems. This includes security breaches, lost data, service outages, workplace injuries and safety hazards.

## Reporting a security incident
Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery. Report by calling the SOC hotline on extension 5999 or by completing form IR-101 on the intranet. Do not try to investigate or fix the problem yourself, and do not delete any evidence.

## Severity levels
Incidents are classified by the SOC into four levels. P1 is critical, with major outage or confirmed data breach. P2 is high, P3 is medium and P4 is low. The SOC responds to P1 incidents within 15 minutes and to P2 incidents within 1 hour. P3 and P4 incidents are handled within 2 business days.

## Personal data breaches
If an incident involves personal data, the Data Protection Officer must be informed immediately. The company must notify the regulator within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, so a delay in reporting can create legal liability.

## Workplace injuries
Workplace injuries and near misses must be reported to the Safety Officer and HR within 24 hours. In an emergency, call the emergency services first, then notify the Safety Officer.

## After the incident
For every P1 and P2 incident, the incident owner holds a post-incident review within 5 business days. The review documents the timeline, root cause and actions, and the final report is shared with the leadership team within 10 business days.

## Anonymous reporting
Employees may report concerns anonymously through the ethics line on the intranet. The company does not retaliate against anyone who reports an incident in good faith.
