# Northwind Facilities IT Equipment Policy

## Standard issue
Every employee receives a company laptop, a charger and a headset. The standard laptop budget is $1,500. Developers and designers may request a higher-specification model, which must be approved by the IT Manager.

## Refresh cycle
Laptops are replaced every 36 months. A laptop may be replaced earlier only if it is damaged beyond repair or fails a hardware diagnostic run by the IT Service Desk. The IT Manager approves every laptop replacement request, whether scheduled or early.

## Requesting equipment
Requests are made through the IT Service Portal. Standard items are fulfilled within 5 business days. Non-standard requests need line manager approval and are approved by the IT Manager.

## Peripheral allowance
Each employee has a peripheral allowance of $150 per calendar year for items such as a keyboard, mouse or monitor stand. Monitors are provided in the office, and remote employees may request one external monitor.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 4 hours of discovery, by phone on extension 5500. The Service Desk will remotely lock and wipe the device. Theft should also be reported to the police, and the case number given to IT.

## Personal devices
Personal phones may be used for work email only after they are enrolled in the company's mobile device management (MDM) system. Personal laptops are not permitted to access company systems.

## Software
Only software from the approved catalogue may be installed. Requests for new software go through the IT Service Portal and need security review, which takes up to 10 business days.

## Leaving the company
All equipment must be returned within 5 business days of the last working day. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from the final payment, up to its remaining value.
