# Expenses Policy

## Scope
This policy covers everyday business expenses such as meals, office supplies, software subscriptions, client entertainment and mileage. Costs of overnight business trips, such as flights and hotels, are governed by the Travel Policy and are reimbursed under different rules.

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted in ExpenseHub within 30 days of the date of purchase. Claims submitted later than 30 days are rejected unless the Finance Director grants an exception in writing. An itemised receipt is required for every expense above $25. For amounts of $25 or less, a short description is enough.

## Approval limits
The employee's line manager approves claims up to $500. Claims above $500 also need approval from the Finance Director. Employees may not approve their own claims or those of their manager.

## Reimbursement
Approved expense claims are reimbursed by bank transfer within 10 business days of final approval. Reimbursement is made in the currency of the employee's payroll account, using the exchange rate on the purchase date.

## Meals and entertainment
When working away from the usual office for a full day, employees may claim up to $40 per day for meals. Client entertainment is limited to $75 per person and requires approval from the line manager before the event. Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment.

## Mileage
Use of a personal car for approved business purposes is reimbursed at $0.58 per mile. Employees must record the start point, destination and distance of each journey.

## Company cards
Employees with a company card must reconcile their card statement in ExpenseHub by the 5th day of the following month. Unreconciled transactions are deducted from the next salary payment after a written reminder.

## Not reimbursable
Fines, personal items, gym memberships and commuting costs are never reimbursable.
