# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month, so new joiners receive a pro-rated amount. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, and carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited. The company also observes 12 public holidays per year, which are not counted against annual leave.

## Requesting leave
All leave is requested through the HR Portal. Requests for 3 days or fewer need at least 2 working days of notice. Requests longer than 3 days need at least 10 working days of notice. The employee's line manager approves or declines the request within 3 working days. If the request is not answered in that time, the employee should escalate to the HR Business Partner.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick leave must be reported to the line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's certificate is required once an absence lasts more than 3 consecutive days. Sick days do not carry over.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver is entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be started within 12 months of the birth or adoption date, and employees should notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted for personal reasons. It must be approved by the Head of Department, not only the line manager, and it does not accrue annual leave while the employee is away.

## Bereavement leave
Employees receive 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and 2 paid days for other close relatives. No notice period applies, but the employee should inform their manager as soon as possible.
