# Northwind Facilities Travel Policy

## Scope
This policy covers overnight business trips, including flights, trains, hotels, rental cars and travel meals. Everyday expenses that are not part of a business trip are covered by the Expenses Policy, which has different limits and deadlines.

## Booking
All trips must be booked through the TravelDesk portal at least 14 days before departure. Bookings made later than that need written approval from the Head of Department. Employees must not book travel on personal accounts.

## Flights and trains
Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is never reimbursed unless the CEO approves it. Rail travel is preferred for journeys under 4 hours.

## Hotels
The hotel limit is $180 per night. In New York, London and San Francisco the limit is $250 per night. Hotels must be booked through TravelDesk to receive the negotiated corporate rate.

## Travel meals
While travelling, employees may claim a meal allowance of $55 per day, paid as a flat per diem and not requiring receipts.

## Approval
Trips costing up to $2,000 are approved by the line manager. Trips costing more than $2,000 must also be approved by the Head of Department. International trips additionally require a security briefing from the IT Security team before departure.

## Reimbursement
Travel claims must be submitted in TravelDesk within 14 days of returning from the trip. Claims submitted after 14 days are rejected. Approved travel claims are reimbursed within 7 business days. Receipts are required for flights, trains and hotels.

## Cancellations and rental cars
Cancel bookings at least 24 hours before departure to avoid unrecoverable fees. Rental cars are allowed only when public transport is not practical and the line manager has agreed in advance.
