# Onboarding Policy

## Before the first day
HR sends the employment paperwork at least 5 business days before the start date and the employee must return it before day one. IT prepares the laptop and delivers it 2 days before the start date for remote joiners. Accounts for email, chat and the HR Portal are created 1 business day before the start date.

## First day
New joiners arrive at the reception desk at 9:30 on their first day. They receive a building pass, a welcome pack and a meeting with their line manager. The HR team hosts a 60-minute orientation session at 11:00 covering benefits, policies and company values.

## Onboarding buddy
Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from the same team, who is not their manager. The buddy supports the new joiner for the first 90 days and holds a short check-in every week for the first month.

## Mandatory training
Security awareness training must be completed within the first 7 days. Compliance and code-of-conduct training must be completed within 14 days. Role-specific training is planned by the line manager.

## Goals and check-ins
The line manager sets initial goals with the new joiner within the first 2 weeks. Formal check-in meetings take place at 30, 60 and 90 days. The 90-day review is documented and shared with HR.

## Probation
The probation period lasts 3 months. During probation, either side may end employment with 1 week of notice. The line manager confirms completion of probation in writing, and HR then updates the employee record.

## Equipment and access
New joiners should receive all standard equipment as described in the IT Equipment Policy. Any missing equipment or access must be reported to the IT Service Desk in the first week.

## Feedback
HR sends a short onboarding survey after 30 days, and results are reviewed quarterly to improve the process.
